About

Sandeep can only be described as a trailblazer in the world of Invisalign.

He is one of Europe's leading Invisalign Diamond Apex Providers and has created more than 30,000 beautiful new smiles with Invisalign in his private clinics.

From India to Invisalign

Originally from India, Sandeep came to the UK in 1999 and qualified with the GDC in 2000. On the look out for a new challenge, Sandeep bought his first NHS practice in 2003 and set about growing his private dental clinic portfolio.

More than 20 years later, Sandeep now owns a number of NHS practices in Birmingham, and three successful private dental clinic brands; MiSmile, Smmmile and Smile Stylist, all of which are Invisalign-focused.

In 2015, Sandeep launched The MiSmile Network after innumerable requests by other practitioners to learn from his Invisalign business model. It now comprises over 100 practices, and is the only GDP network endorsed by Align Technology.

Never one to stand still, in 2022, Sandeep founded 'Mastering your Invisalign Business'; a one-day immersive course designed to show practices how to master their own Invisalign business.

Sandeep loves to share his passion for Invisalign with the wider industry and can often be found on stages around the globe delivering lectures, presentations and workshops to share his passion and secrets with others.

"A successful man is one who can lay a firm foundation with the bricks others have thrown at him." — A favourite quote of Dr Sandeep Kumar
